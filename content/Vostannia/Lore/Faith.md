Faith. 

Church System 

A God holds authority over a concept in the form of a Godhead– the crystallization of their understanding. They are the embodiment of a concept, but they are not the concept themselves. The faith that sustains the God of Life may ebb and flow, but Life as a concept is timeless. 

So how do followers fit into this? A Godhead is akin to an engine, and you need fuel for that engine to run. Worship creates Divinity, which in turn sustains this God. By worshiping a God, these followers gain access to the framework that this God created. 

To progress, they must build up their understanding of the God's authority– the lens in which He perceives this concept. If I were a follower of the God of Life, then I would have to understand his perspective to advance. Now, this doesn't mean that a person can't derive something different from their God. While I don't have a concrete plan for the lower and intermediate stages, a follower will eventually be able to create fragments of a Godhead, to create their own authority and become a God. 

Ofcourse, as one would expect– two tigers can't share a tiger. If two Gods of Life exist, then the old God would lose divinity as some of his followers would defect to the new God of Life. Their authorities would inherently conflict, forcing them to devour each other. In this case, the Newcomer won. 

So what happens to the Old God? Most of the time, they get devoured in full and die– but there's always an exception. If they manage to stay alive and retain a part of their Godhead, they become dormant as they wait for a chance to condense new fragments and become a God again. In this Era, they lost– but what about the next era? The neat part about losing your Godhood is that you can move more freely in the mortal world. 

Ideas I have related to this: 
- Derived concepts. For example, Healing as a concept would be derived from Life. This would introduce a hierarchy between Derived Godheads and Fundamental Godheads and ensure that every time a follower creates their Godhead, they aren't devoured immediately. 

There's more that I have for this idea, but I'll develop it more later. 

Ancestral Worship
The second branch of faith that I want to introduce is Ancestor worship, an idea that could work well with Ash's idea of the Sigillate-King.

This type of faith is hereditary in nature, so as a consequence it's more exclusive. Like the name would suggest, an entire bloodline would worship their Ancestor instead of a God. Like Gods, this worship would sustain him. Blood would serve as a medium for his power and understanding to be transmitted to his descendants, either in part or in full. This depends on their bloodline concentration. 

Now, the key distinction between these two systems is that an Ancestor doesn't need Godhood for his system to exist. At the same, members of this bloodline would find it almost impossible to surpass the peak of this Ancestor– his knowledge serving as a boon for the mediocre and a curse for the talented. 

An idea that I have with this system to make it more unique is that multiple ancestors can exist and ensure that their understanding is passed down, even if it skips a few generations. If we're going to use this, then you guys decide if it should be implemented or not.

I'm not writing a Mary sue here, so this system's weakness is its lack of members and fragility. If anything happens to the inheritance, if the more talented successors keep on dying, if the bloodline gets diluted, if the family gets assimilated with a church system, it will decline and die out completely. 

These are the two ideas I wanted to share, so thanks for reading my TED talk.
