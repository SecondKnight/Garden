Hello again my beloved patrons of the LNN. At the behest of our dictator, here I am covering this story for you at 0500– I'd be damned if y'all aren't reading this. As many of you know, we established a new nation to erase the controversies that previously bound us. But, as the Successor of the Chaos Legion– we've inherited some of their original sins. The original sin that I refer to here is none other than 2SKEP. A man who once led us to glory, now one that bears the banner of the Stratocracy. Be it the carcass of Chaos or the body of the Directorate, there is no redemption for treason.

I'm not known for my artistic flair, so let me paint this picture with words. 2SKEP first joined the Legion back in 2021 and distinguished himself in the Chicago Incursion– an event that elevated him straight to Epilochias. Later that year, he furthered his reputation by reinstating the Ministry of Truth– serving as one of our top reporters. Even as time passes, our commitment stands true– truth itself. Nothing less. Eventually he would take a break in June of 2023 and make his return next March after receiving his promotion as Legion Commander. Those of you familiar with 2SKEP probably remember the W incursion he led in 2024– the first encounter we've had with that genre. In June of 2025, he parted ways again, joining the Noobic Stratocracy– torching his portrait with the flame of ambition.  

I can't write for other communities, but Vostannia has a solid grip of those that once belonged to us. Members of the Old Guard come back here like it's an abusive relationship, and it seems that 2SKEP is no exception here. He proposed another incursion in Windward and roped the Stratocracy into this expedition, a past controversy erupting– a sight that did not please the Officer Corps. 

Various debates were held with multiple votes before his plan was green-lit. After that, he was reinstated and was promptly subject to the watching gaze of all of the officers, a claim that almost exaggerates the amount of attention we gave him– some of us just needed a reason. Today, these officers are vindicated. 

The man who proposed the Second Windward Incursion has dropped out. 2SKEP claimed that he "had a plethora of reasons" but didn't think they'd be interesting, so he went quietly into the night– or at least he tried. But 2SKEP is an honorable man, these reasons must be sound– just as Brutus was an honorable man. After all, no honorable commander would leave out of boredom– so surely his hand was forced. 

Now, Captain Dino is now responsible for our efforts in the Windward Incursion. As for the Stratocracy, we shall see– if they'll keep their word or if they pull back. They can't be blamed here due to these changing circumstances. For the time being, this is still a developing topic. I write with the knowledge I have, so I could be wrong– that this truth is just a shard of the picture. But, the Ministry can't withhold the truth out of uncertainty– this is all I have for you today. I forgot my signature in the Airship Assault battle, but I won't forget it this time. As always, thanks for tuning in! This is the LNN, signing off!
