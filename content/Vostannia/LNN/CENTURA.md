Hello again my beloved patrons of the LNN. With just a week before the W incursion starts again, Ehrenwache has claimed another victory against the Noobic Stratocracy in Centura, a well-known third-person-shooter game. As the Network didn't cover the first battle, I'll give the run-down for the game itself and a summary of our previous battle.

Centura's time period is set in the World Wars with rifles that tend to one-shot most enemies. Apart from that, it has multiple classes: Medics with their bandages, Officers with their whistles, Anti-tank equipped with RPGS, Sweepers with a shotgun and sword, along with more classes. So far, we fought on multiple maps with two common gamemodes– objectives & team deathmatches. Deathmatches work similarly to the game Gravedigger, with both teams receiving a set amount of lives. The first team to run out of lives loses. 

Last month, we fought them with both sides given 24 tanks. 12 anti-infantry tanks and 12 tank-killers. Unlike most games, players have almost no control over them– so our job was to suppress their tanks and seize objective points. Two jobs that we blitzed through as we destroyed half of their tanks and pushed them back to their spawn, taking control of all five objective points. While most of us were excited for round 2, the Stratocracy forfeited the second round entirely so their commander could enjoy the remnants of their birthday. It's a shame that they scheduled it on such a jolly day. 

Ironically, today's battle was our Commander's birthday– so we gave Axolde his gift early with another victory. In the first round, we split into two components and quickly took over three objectives before the Noobs stalled us for a bit. After we slowly pushed through, we took their last objective and started to bomb them with artillery– winning the first round in just 12:22 minutes. 

For the second round, we played a deathmatch against the noobs in a trench map. What made this map unique was its poison gas. If you take any damage, then your gas mask breaks– slowly killing you. Since we only had a limited amount of lives, we used medics to extend our lives and moved in groups. For a good part of the match, we flanked them and continued to trap them in their spawn. 

But, we only had a few minutes before midnight struck to give our present. It wouldn't look good for us to be a minute left. Once they had a few lives left, we charged into their spawn and bashed their heads open. Once again, Ehrenwache reigns supreme against the Noobics and their Vietnamese tactics.  While we have a lot of upcoming events coming up, that's all I have for you guys. As always, thanks for tuning in! This is the LNN, signing off!
