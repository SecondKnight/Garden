Hello again beloved patrons of the LNN. Even as the Legionary Assembly inches forward, the Directorate's titans are still at it with their heated rivalry– even if it's at the top of a summit. Within the last two weeks, the Combined Arms Battalion and Ehrenwache sent out multiple climbing parties to conquer Mount Godwin-Austen to uphold the honor of their divisions. Even as the mountain's feet becomes littered with their bodies, these members are determined to show that their legion is the strongest.

When it comes to climbing this mountain, it is by far a pain in the ass. Mount Godwin-Austin, also known as K2, takes roughly 1-2 hours to fully climb if you don't fall. There is no natural regeneration for your health, and like everything else in our capitalist society– healthcare is expensive. This game has three camps that serve as checkpoints, but the neat part is that they aren't respawn anchors. Once you inevitably die, you have to start from the beginning unless you've been embezzling robux from the divisional coffers. The teams start at 5300m with the submit at an altitude of 8500m

Ehrenwache made the first move when they set out on the 6th. Axolde quickly mobilized the Ehrenwache Mountain Expeditionary Unit to conquer K2 on. The team of 12 set out against the Mountain, but it appears that they've underestimated this force of nature. Unlike CAB who had previous experience climbing this mountain, these men only had faith in their Grand Marshal– but that wasn't enough. All 12 members of this expedition died by the time they reached 6500m, their bodies becoming one with the mountain. Even in death, their ears still ring. The voice of Eive, Ehrenwache's Oberst-Ajdjutant could be heard thousands of miles away as he raged with all of his might.

On the 7th, CAB followed up afterwards even after looking at these gruesome deaths– claiming the first victory in this war. Their climbing party had 10 people with Veronica gracing the team with her presence as General. Unfortunately, the General had to leave early and transfer leadership over to Zack after receiving a transmission from Headquarters. The Logistics building caught on fire following unverified reports of a suit-clad cat entering the building with a jerry can shortly before the incident.

After this first loss, the rest of the team followed Zack's leadership as he navigated them through the guided path– a path designed for beginners with red lights guiding parts of their path. While it may not be the quickest, it's the safest one. Unfortunately, that wasn't enough to keep everyone alive. During this expedition, the snow blocked the team's vision as Ariel stumbled and fell into a narrow gap. He survived the initial fall but inertia kept him going as he slid off– reaching terminal velocity as he joined fallen 12 in solidarity. In the end, only 8 reached the summit– the first battle won.

Within a week, Ehrenwache redeployed the Mountain Expeditionary Unit with 3 additional members, turning it into a team of 15. Axolde led this team with Nilhilus guiding our paths. His name may be ‘nothing’, but his presence was a wall– one that pushed us forward. Unlike CAB, Ehrenwache paved the way expeditiously as they split off into smaller groups– taking multiple routes. As a result, only 7 made it up to the summit after taking the Icefall path– skipping checkpoints in the process. 

Officially, Martan is the only casualty on this expedition. Unfortunately, his dress froze in this harsh weather and he wasn't able to dodge the debris from the avalanche. Unofficially, Me and Potato met our demise after taking the 'Buffalo Orophile' route, a path that skips all checkpoints and is by far the steepest out of all the routes taken by any group– peaking at 7800m. As for the rest, their fates are unknown– presumed missing in action. Dead or alive, the snow covers their bodies.

As of writing this story, CAB is in the lead when it comes to the morality rate and Ehrenwache has secured the completion time. Now, we wait for one of these categories to be snatched by these two titans– or by the other divisions. Praetoria and LTF6 haven't joined the fray yet, but only time can tell if they too take up arms in this race. This is only the start, as both divisions haven't tapped deep into their reserves yet. But for now, that's all we have for you today. As always, thanks for tuning in! This is the LNN, signing off!
