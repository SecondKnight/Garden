Hello again my beloved patrons of the LNN. July's almost over, but the paper press is still hot. Recently, CAB held their monthly assembly and gave us a slew of topics to report as they droned on for a good thirty minutes. Goombert finally retired from his position as Mareşal of the 7th Legionary Engineering Company (7LEC) and gave it to MRSL. For those of you unfamiliar with this sub-division, 7LEC is responsible for artillery support, from Napoleonic Warfare to Modern Combat. You'll see them more often in the upcoming battles.

While most of us were expecting Goombert to nuke the server, he did something even worse– he unretired. It seems that he can only rest when the Battalion's holes are patched, given their recent departures. Veronica was mortified as she watched him waltz back onto the stage, for him to announce his new spot as 2IC of the 18th. After hearing this, she walked off-stage for a few minutes. Witnesses heard the sound of metal and muffled cries before a sudden crack subdued further noise. After she walked back onto the stage, Sammy walked behind her– limping as he used a bent crowbar as a crutch.

Just as Sammy couldn't find it in himself to chuckle, the rest of the battalion quickly lost their humor as Veronica spoke of an investigation. One that targeted the entire battalion, scouring for members that partook in "condo" games and other illicit activities. In the Assembly itself, she struck four members off the registry, setting an example. The rest of the Officers moved quickly after the assembly and spent two days investigating. In total, 19 members were found and expelled– in both name and spirit.

These games tend to evade legal scrutiny given their short lifespans, but I can't say the same for their players. With the entire world moving towards a surveillance state, just wash your wrists– don't dirty those cuffs when you finally wear them. People think that time will conceal their faults, when it just collects them– waiting for somebody to find them. While Vostannia isn't the best nation, our true decline would start when such dubious members fester in our ranks and rise above their pit in hell. After this Assembly, an anonymous report form and disciplinary database was established– ensuring greater accountability. I could write another thousand words on this, but that'd taint my duty. So for now, that's all I have for you guys. As always, thanks for tuning in! This is the LNN, signing off!
