Hello again beloved patrons of the LNN. Today, I'll be covering a topic that's a bit more digestible for you all. After all, I've only been writing heavy hitters since my tenure began as a writer.

Our Grand Marshal– MrAshApple, has always been one to believe that the means of production belong to the people. To call him a leftist would be an understatement, but the Directorate welcomes all– provided that they respect the sanctity of human life. 

While I can't write much for the main Directorate, his will is no different than the collective will of the divisions. They may have their differences, but he moved like Earl Warren to write an unanimous statement that spoke for every officer that this Directorate has sired. 

For a Marxist, he has better clothes than me– perhaps he could distribute some for the rest of us. Then again, even our uniforms were textured by his hands alone. Some would shrug this off as honey-wrapped words, but which one of these feats would you deny?

My words come in layers and this story is no different. Recently, Ehrenwache Officers and staff members have been posting the results of their political compass tests. While their behaviour can be written off as a humorous way to learn more about each other, it gives us a look into the political leanings of Ehrenwache as a whole. Even without the Marshal's influence, how far do these men bend? 

Oberleutent Boculus started this trend off when he claimed that his compass was normal, inadvertently taunting the rest of the officer corps to post the results of their test. While his results indicate that he is a leftist libertarian, he appears to be a centrist with a slight lean towards the left. 

Stabsfeldwebel Zack_Rofan immediately responded with his own results. While many of us expected him to a rightward authoritarian, we were only half-right. It seems that this man is also a leftist. It goes to show that some of us may speak right but act left.  

Hauptfeldwebel Air is true to his name, as his results show that he's the most libertarian out of us all. Ofcourse, his wind blows to the left like the rest of us. It's a bit ironic that we're all expecting each other to be right. 

Großmajor Husking came in second place after Air when it comes to being free. The amount of jokes I can write from this is ludicrous, but the red tape of bureaucracy is going to strangle my ink if I try it. It's not like he's the head of my department or anything like that.

Stabsfeldwebel MrVery1 differed from his counterpart by being a libertarian. I say that their natures are both balanced, like newton's third law. Quite frankly, it's surprising that this man isn't a radical authoritarian. But, tests aren't always accurate. 

Speaking of which, Stabsfeldwebel TheDanGame appears to be the only man whose beliefs are moderate. He neither believes in freedom too much or too little, yet he serves the divisions as military police. A man who lives free yet enforces a system that stifles it, an interesting sight to see.

It seems that we have too many Stabsfeldwebels, which makes my task of writing titles a bit easier. At the same time, perhaps it's time for some of them to get promotions. Stabsfeldwebel Crusader_Lord neither believes in freedom nor in authority, a textbook leftist who definitely center-left if you look at it one way. 

GeneralAdjucant Yeetus did his best to be a rightward authoritarian, but ended up as another statistic in this story– a leftist libertarian. As for the rankers, Gato is a true centrist, Axoide who decided who post in ranker chat is similar to Crusader_Lord. 

This ending may have been a bit rushed, but it wasn't written seriously to begin with. From the very start, we all knew that this test was biased when not a single man came out as a righty. I sound right, but it is only by the virtue of my tongue.

As a reader, being skeptical is akin to sharpening a blade. If you sharpen it too hard, you'll spill your own blood. If you don't sharpen it at all, then you'll get stabbed. I feel like I haven't teased these guys enough, but there's always another story I can write up.

For now, that's all I have for you guys. As always, thanks for tuning! This is the LNN, signing off!
