# Road Guard Procedure & Transitory Drill
 

## Road Guards:

- 2-4 Cadets responsible for signaling traffic while the Flight crosses the road. Flight Commander typically assigns them a number and calls them as such. Example: "Road Guard 1,3 OUT"
- Commands: (Speed is key)
	- Don Gear: Wear equipment, typically executed before Road Guard performs their duties.
	- Out: Guards take up 'Parade Rest' positions on the road, with their right arm out at 90 degrees to signal incoming traffic.
	- In: Guards return to their original spots in the formation **with their arms down.**
- Formations:
	- Two Guards in the front, two in the back: Typically used with four guards
	- Guards in the back: Flag Guards stay in the back of the formation.

## Flight Commander:

- Responsible for coordinating Flight and Road Guard movements and signaling traffic when Road Guards are positioning themselves. 
- Is the first person to walk onto the road and the last person to leave it.
- Evaluates safety conditions and yields to any incoming traffic until the road is clear. 
- Commands should only consist of column movements and Forward March. 
- Does facing movements towards the Flight to give the proper commands.


### Procedure: 

Flight Commander marches into the middle of the road, looks at both sides of the road, check safety conditions, puts both arms out at 90 degrees. They proceed to do an above-face with their arms down and then puts their arms back up to signal traffic to stop. They then call Road Guard out with this verbage 'Road Guard #, # OUT'

Flight Commander then goes into Attention to give commands to the flight. The only commands that should be called is **Forward March and Column movements.** Column movements become necessary for more complex crossings. Flight Commander remains at attention until the flight passes. 

After the Flight crosses, they will command the Flight to halt. After doing so, they will recall Road Guards with the following verbage 'Road Guard #, # IN'. Flight Commander then crosses the road after both Guards return to formation. 

If an incident occurs where ongoing traffic fails to yield, yell 'Knock it off' and take immediate action to safeguard yourself.
