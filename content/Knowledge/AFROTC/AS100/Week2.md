# Monday: F45

Monday was an interesting day. I forgot what workout we did, so I'll write it off as "Firestorm". It's the only name that rang a bell for me. Either way, it definitely deserves it's reputation– I did 30 reps until I had to take a break. It was just like my PFRA with the 2-mile run, me reaching the tolerance with my nausea. Unfortunately, I miscalculated and actually threw up this time around. 

But, it's not my first time vomiting nor shall it be my last. This is acceptable as I've never made a real effort to exercise and train– my body's condition being a result of this. All I need is time, which is something akin to a paradox. I have time, but I don't have enough of it. But, this journal isn't to contemplate on life's metaphysical qualities. 

To remedy this, I need to train **regularly**. I can do a mock PFRA for my push ups daily. For sit-ups, I need to find something to support my weight– at either Ping or in my room itself. In regards to running, it's more of a quagmire since I can't strain my legs too much. I already cover 3 miles daily just by cycling back and forth between classes. So if I screw up with my legs, it affects everything.

I'll look into high-intensity running for a set duration per day? More research needs to be done before I commit to an actual improvement plan. There's also the nutritionist but the results of that are for Week 3. 

# Tuesday: Swim PT

Was slightly late to the Aquatic Center since I was unfamiliar with the entrance. For the most part, it was a lot less stressful than normal PT. I know that I won't drown and it takes less energy than normal physical training. In exchange, I get to feel cold and have to wash my clothes again. 

# Wednesday: Rappel?

Originally we were supposed to have our morale event with the Army ROTC event, but that got cancelled thanks to the heat. That worked out for me since I used that time to get into regulations. I've also come to the realization that I should only rely on the overview emails for our classes, since there was no event at Walter Hall. It would've been nice to know beforehand, but oh well. I'd rather show up than to accidently miss an event. 


# Thursday: Water Survival 

Water Survival was fun. I got the newer lifejacket so I didn't have to worry about drowning. But, it still took me a couple minutes to figure out a way to move myself instead of drifting around. I can definitely say that it's not fun colliding with people and trying to not screw my fellow cadets over by getting in their way. 

Other than that, all three trainings were nice. I thought about opting out of the parachute training but didn't after watching a demonstration. It's the easiest one for me since you tug on the parachute and use it to propel yourself forward. Sure, you can waterboard yourself– but only if you barely lift it up. While it was tempting to try again with people throwing water, I'm not going to test my luck. 

After this, I had my headshot photo and my meeting with C/Renkas– one that I had to reschedule since I missed my original time. While I did expect some generic questions, the one that went along the lines "Who's your wingman?" was interesting. <s>While I'd love to go into more detail, this journal is designated for my AFROTC experiences, so further discussion would detract from the subject at hand. </s> 

When it comes to human relationships, I categorize and define them strictly. The cadets in my cohort are either friend or acquaintances. Friends if I enjoy their company and acquaintances if I haven't had that pleasure yet. I would define a wingman as a person that I interact with regularly, a person that I can trust and rely on– a person that doesn't exist. I have no wingman, but the record probably doesn't reflect that reality. After all, my rejection was implicit the moment I said "The people **closest** to that... "

 Now when it comes those above me, let me quote my own words here.

>"People of authority are nothing new, so I have my own ways of dealing with them– be it good or bad. In the future I see for myself, their authority will become weights that'll bear heavy on my crown. I haven't met them yet, but I know. I also know that I can't discern good or bad so soon, so why would I give myself away? Prudence is a virtue, one that I'll keep. Water flows and winds shift as I adapt to these nuances, but I can't have others reading that. That'd be rigging the scales against me. So if I can't write it, I'll speak it."

# Friday: F45 

Tried to fix my attendance by sleeping early. In exchange, I woke at 0600 sharp– the most late I've ever been so far. This is definitely a great look for my standing within the detachment, and will surely not bite me in my next meeting with C/Renkas. I still need to find his actual rank for further correspondence. 

Anyways I reached Ping by 0608 by madly cycling my way on the road. When I'm not running lake, I bike on sidewalks during dusk and dawn. That way, I don't turn into roadkill enroute. This workout was easier than Monday's, so I completed all 45 reps and still had enough energy to bike back to Nelson. 

I ate nine loafs of bread, a good amount. One day, I shall eat an entire bag. With the end of that breakfast, came the end of this week.

Within the next two meetings, I plan on pitching this idea of a journal archive to C/Renkas– provided that I have enough time. After all, I'm a busy man.
