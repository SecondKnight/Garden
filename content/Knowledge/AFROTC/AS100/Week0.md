## Introduction

Greetings Cadets, be it my Juniors, my Peers, or my Seniors. I am C/Warsame, responsible for every word written into this record. As I write this introduction, I am currently an AS100– but the goal of this is far beyond my years. 

As I have no plans to willingly leave this program, I will inevitably fall into a position to mentor the cadets that come after me. At the same time, I want to document my experience here as a way for me to visit the past. So, let this record be established as a window– a window that we all can look back at. 

I'm not worried about my flaws being shown. What I am worried about is failing to maintain a clean image. Not a positive one, but a clean one. I will not embellish, nor shall I disparage anything with my words. After all, this is supposed to be a window into **my** past– not a distortion. 

## Why AFROTC?

Out of all of the paths I saw, it was the best available choice for the future I envisioned for myself.  As a student, I would prefer graduating with a minimal amount of debt. If I were to graduate normally, then I have no faith in trusting the job market. After all, my cohort won't be the only ones gunning for an entry-level position. For some reason, we've gone to the point where **everyone** wants it– forcing us to compete with PHDs and Masters. 


Apart from that, joining the Military is a great way for me to kickstart my Cybersecurity degree. Now, if I'm going to join this program– then I have milk it for all its worth. In exchange for these four years, I'm staying for twelve. Ofcourse, I may stay for less or more– but that's the goal. There's also the benefits you get from your service, some being lifelong.
