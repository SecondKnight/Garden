## AS100

- Field Training
	- may be two locations instead of just the base in Alabama. 
	- Get to shoot military weapons.
	- hands-on training
	- Goal of FT is to assess whether a cadet is suitable to enter the POC core. 
- Scholarships 
	- In-College Scholarships
		- ISCP Phases 1 & 2
			- Highly competitive, against other detachments. 
			- Targets 1st and 2nd year students.
			- Requires students to be **medically** cleared before disbursement.
		- CMLA 
			- Award that covers two years of schooling after you complete FT
			- Can divert it to housing with a limit of $10,000 per year, requires you to stay in college-provided housing.
