## Core values 

- ### Why is it important to live by core values?
	- It gives you direction and helps guide your life as a leader. It essentially serves as a rubric that we use to assess our situation.  
- ### Main points 
	- What it means to be an airman or guardian
	- Core values of an airman
	- Core values of an guardian. 

Definition of **Airman**

>  An enlisted person in the air force below the rank of sergeant. Quoted from some dictionary that is incorrect as it is more broad than just the enlisted corps. Airman is any person that was once associated with the USAF, military or civilian.

While this definition defines the meaning of airman, it tells us nothing of what we're supposed to be– be it as Guardians or as Airmen. 
  It does not tell us what it means about who we are as guardians or airmen. 

## Profession of Arms

Patriots who protect the constitution and are held accountable to ethical and performance standards. They possess common attributes of character, courage, and competence. They also abide by service core values. 


- Characteristics of Airmen 
	- Leadership 
	- Understanding of airpower

- Characteristics of Guardians 
	- Principled public servants
	- space-minded warfighters
	- bold and collaborative problem solvers. 

**Note: You will have to memorize the Airman's creed eventually**

**Core values** serve as the bedrock of leadership in both USAF and USSF. These institutional values act as principles of conduct, providing a moral framework for military activities– an enduring one that remains fundamental to this very day. 

Virtues are defined by the USAF as "desired behaviors and characteristics that we must practice and demonstrate". Oxford has a more general definition, that virtues are quality that are considered morally good or desirable.  `Remember to add your own definition and link the relevent essay`



## USAF Core Values: Integrity, Service, Excellence.

- ### Integrity First 
	- Define with own words:
	- Single most important part of character
	- to do the right thing, all the time. 
	- moral compass that keeps us on the right path
	- The foundation upon which trust is built.
	- Thoughts and action that align with what is right. 
	- Virtues
		- Honesty
			- This is the only way to preserve the trust we hold so dear with each other and with the population we serve.
		- Courage
			- Willingness to take personal and professional risks, make decisions that may be unpopular, and admit to our mistakes
		- Accountability
		- Humility
- ### Service Before Self
	- Define with own words
	- Professional duties take precedence over personal desires. 
	- Call to live to a higher standard 
	- Commitment that takes energy, dedication, and sacrifice. 
	- Servant mindset
	- Embrace the expectations and requirements of the profession of arms. 
	- TSGT Goode's story
		- Wife was six months pregnant, was deployed to Niger. Had the opportunity to come back for a few weeks to see birth of child. Chose to stay as he was the only one who could do postal work for the base. 
	- Virtues
		- Duty
			- Consistently choosing to make necessary sacrifices to accomplish the mission and, in doing so, honoring those who make the ultimate sacrifice. 
		- Loyalty
			- Commitment to the success and preservation of something bigger than ourselves. 
			- Demonstrated by helping each act with honor. 
		- Respect
			- Treating others with dignity and valuing them as individuals. 
- ### Excellence in all we do
	- Directs us to continually advance our craft and increase our knowledge as Air Force professionals 
	- Passion for continuous improvement and innovation to propel accomplishment and performance.
	- On a personal level, we seek out and complete developmental education; work to stay in their best physical, mental, and moral shape. 
	- Virtues
		- Mission
			- A complex undertaking requiring a focus encompassed with operations, product, and resource excellence. 
		- Discipline
			- Commitment to uphold the highest of personal and professional standards 
		- Teamwork
			- Recognizing the interdependency of every member's contributions towards the mission and striving for organizational excellence.
