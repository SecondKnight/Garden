### Active Shooter

Received a briefing on what we're supposed to do with active shooters. After that, we split up into teams and went to different rooms. My team, Bravo-1 was sent to the flight simulator room before the drill started. The table football table is really good for blocking the door, but I believe that we could've done better.

If the suspect is already aware of our presence in the room, then it's better for us to keep the lights on for us to communicate across the room via hand signals. In addition to this, I doubt that the doors are thick enough to block bullets. While holding the table in place, we lowered ourselves– but it's also risky to do that. After all, it's not like the suspect only has one shot. Instead of going for the head, he may shoot at the middle to hit our center mass and instead get our heads due to our lowered positions. 

I believe that six people should be the maximum for holding the table:
