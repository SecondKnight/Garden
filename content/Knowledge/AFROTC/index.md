This folder is the collection of what I've written about the Air Force Reserves Officer Training Corps (AFROTC). Originally, I made this folder to store my journal logs, but its original purpose has now expanded since I have enough time. 

I plan on expanding this for as long as I remain in the program– be it days, weeks, months, years. After all, I can't tell– I can't see four years into the future. If I blindly believe that I'll make it through, then it's arrogance on my part. Regardless, I will strive to do my best. If I don't make it– I'll just maintain it or hand it off to someone else. 

So without further ado, check out the notes I've written. 
- If you want access to my weekly logs, **then just ask.** The reason why I have them here is to give future cadets an idea of the program. 
- Technical notes are public since there's no confidential information on it. 
	- I have my ways of recording passwords if I ever progress into that stage. 
- Delta FT notes are locked to members of the flight. **Again, ask me.**
