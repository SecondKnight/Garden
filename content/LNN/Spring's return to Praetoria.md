Even as the Legion News Network went through its own reforms, we didn't close our eyes for a second. Now that we're back up and running, I can write about some changes happening far from home. Recently, Praetoria welcomed one of their former Legates back into the division, a man that some of you know well– Springster. True to his name, he made the division bloom before he got scorched by summer   

Before he joined us, he built up a garden of experience in the Greek Peninpelago– waging war against city-states. He roamed around and collected their greatest achievements. In Rome, the King awarded a gold laurel for his leadership against Sparta. In addition to this, he grew his own corps of officers, giving him an edge when it came to large scale rallies. During a later campaign, he led Athens and Sparta against Rome. A decisive victory against 140 with just the strength of a hundred. Throughout his career, he repeatedly snatched victory from the jaws of defeat– crushing his foes and their superior numbers. 

So when Spring rejoined Praetoria, his allies joined him without missing a beat– Archmenes & Blitz. Blitz priding himself on being a duelist, one that sharpened his sword on the Sparta's finest. Archmendes being a commander that previously worked with Spring on several campaigns. With their experience being infused into Praetoria, only time can tell how this will change the division. 

After Spring's return, he first apologized for his absence before he announced a new Greek campaign– one that merged both Lake and Directional mechanics. Along with this campaign, he promised consistency in events and rallies. By the end of the year, his goal is to see the Division rally fifty men for its battle– but that task is one for an entire division, not just him. We'll see if Praetoria can match his commitment, but for now? That's all I have for my beloved readers. As always, thanks for tuning in! This is the LNN, signing off! Ave Praetoria!
