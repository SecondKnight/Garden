Hello again beloved patrons of the LNN. Today is not just Spain's victory, it is OURS. For the past two weeks, there's been an Airship Assault internal brewing between Ehrenwache and the Combined Arms Battalion. Strategies were made, teams were drafted, plans were enacted, documents were legislated. But, time wasn't on our side– not on mine as I'm writing this paper nor the Divisions. So, the internal was changed to a mixed one to decide the final roster for yesterday's battle– a crucible that melted down into 32 players. 

It was these 32 that annihilated the Noobic Stratocracy's 1st Noobic Fleet, Noobic Marine Corps, Noobic Naval Aerial Corps in two battles. In the battle, our fighters split into three groups to optimize their performance: Defenders, Raiders, and the Pilots. The Defenders were split into two squadrons, one focused on repairing components– led by Potatoknight. The second squadron was responsible for operating the armaments and defending our glorious airship from Noobic raiders. 

The pilots management was more simple, with the cap being 3– captainace was responsible for this trio. Raiders were similarly split into two groups of people. Seal Team 6 was responsible for destroying the airship by targeting the boiler room and airship pillars connecting the ship with the balloon– this team being led by Axoide. The Harassment squad's job was to make the enemy crew's lives a living hell by camping teleporters, killing gunners, and sabotaging MG nests– all of this devised by Husking. Even their captain wasn't left alone, my shotgun burning their skins multiple times as they slumped over– to ensure that this hell was an inclusive one.

The first battle was won by breaking all of the pillars off of the Noobic Airship, a sight that delighted us as they plummeted towards their destined deaths. While our commander Husking may excel in writing documents and devising plans, his technological prowess lags behind to the Stratocracy's lament. We enjoyed an unofficial second round fighting over an objective before Husking figured out how to change the map, depriving me of the airship I stole from them. 

As for the actual second round, our strategies remained the same– we didn't feel enough of a challenge to adjust. However, I'll give them credit for the second round. Their machine gunners were a pest for our raiding delegations, shooting multiple men down before they could reach the airship. It didn't help that they covered both the boiler room and the captain's room with bodies. I kid you not, this feat of theirs single-handedly dragged this round out longer than any of us expected. They also figured out not all noobs are allies when I changed my skin tone to match theirs for easier access to their captain. 

But, their struggle was no avail. Victory was ours when Boculus threw a grenade into their left boiler, crippling the airship. I pray that these noobs learnt how to swim from their experience from last time, but perhaps CAB may have to do some clean-up operations. Spain may have needed 20 shots to secure their win today, but we did it with just two– Glory to Legionary Vostannia.
