Just as our members are making moves in their lives, the Divisions haven’t faltered. Once again, Praetoria has found a new genre to dedicate themselves to after the sudden retirement of Sniper. The Division was given two choices when it came to their future: to continue the fight in ‘Swords & Sigils’ under a different banner, or to fight in ‘The Tarnished Age’ [TTA] as World Eaters as a sequel to our first incursion. We already fought once as the World Bearers, spreading Logar’s truth– so this vote was a landslide, 10 in favor with 3 against. 

We ran roughshod against the Imperium last year, until the development team decided to break our knees with their idea of balancing– so we left unsated. We’re back to collect on this blood debt, and I want YOU to join our efforts. Some of you already had some experience in this game since KC used to host late-night raids whenever he needed men. TTA’s combat system is reminiscent of Deepwoken with the melee, so you’ll want to watch your rhythm– don’t bleed out too soon.  As we get more active in the coming days, you’ll learn the ropes either in training or in the midst of battle.

This time around, we should be stable with this change. So I ask that you answer the call of the Division. It’s summer, so let your time be used for something more meaningful than waiting for the Directorate to host another raid. After all, the Divisions are expanding with Ehrenwache leading the charge in Khorn and Praetoria with TTA– so make a move of your own. That’s all I have for you today. As always, thanks for tuning in! This is the LNN, signing off!
