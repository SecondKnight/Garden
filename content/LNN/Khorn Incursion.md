Now, the Directorate has blown them oncemore in Windward– not to win, but to thoroughly avenge what was once the Legion. A good part of us still remember those days when we had an entire division dedicated to this, one led by Captain Dino. This is a chance for them to relive their glory days and for us to inherit this struggle. 

Now, let me paint a picture of this game and your roles in it since the announcements covered the rest. Windward is an open-world naval game set in the Napoleonian era. After you enlist in 'Khorn', your in-game skills will be categorized into merits. There are four merits: Gunnery, Sailing, Carpentry, Combat. Each of merits unlock a different progression path for you in the ranking system, with the Officer route requiring proficiency in all merits. 

Gunnery resides at Gun Deck, deals with cannons, and serves as the main offence until we get close enough to board. They are led by a Harbinger who marks locations and directs cannon fire. Carpentry resides at Orlop deck and serves as the maintenance and repair branch, they're responsible for making sure we don't sink and have sufficient armor. They are led by a Karpentry Officer. Sailing is the most complex one due to the game's mechanics, they either assist the captain or man the ships themselves. Combat serves as our boarding party and is assigned elsewhere until they come into play.
