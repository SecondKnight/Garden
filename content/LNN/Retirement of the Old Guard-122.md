Hello again beloved patrons of the LNN. Chaos may be our defining trait, but it's our job to sift through this carnage and write the truth. This time around, we're here to announce the departures of some of the old guard. 

After a strong tenure of five years, Tuner has taken his leave amongst us. He's been a constant presence for us all during these times, and he'll be missed. I've been reading through logs and he's always been locked in for the Directorate with the amount of game nights and events he hosted. 

It was only after he left that I realized that he's a bit of a tsundere. In his retirement announcement, he claimed that the larp wasn't that serious, yet he spent a good five years of his life here. His words speak one truth while his actions speak another. 

His last sentence asked for us to forge our own ending, and so we shall. It's not like our founding was anything less. Six years ago, did we not win the Civil War that determined our fate? We wrote our beginning and we shall certainly write our own end. 

He wrote this and it's our turn to write the rest of these pages. Don't forget, when one falls– another takes their place. No matter how many times this pen switches hands, it is ours for the keeping. May his future days fare better than the joy he had with us.

In my last story about Praetoria, I covered SniperRothward's endeavors with House Stark. Unfortunately, he burnt himself out with this final project of his. As such, he is retiring from the community and from Roblox in general. At the time of writing this story, he left the Directorate and Division servers both.

He may have went quietly into the night, but he rose up here like the Sun's first rays. He had this moment and wove it into something greater than himself. As a friend of Praetoria and of the Directorate itself, we wish him the best. I've been juggling Directorate work for a solid minute and it does take a toll. 

As for the last one, I believe that I have to give you guys some background here. RCL is a Roblox combat system that serves as one of the purest expressions of skill. The simplest way to describe it would be to just move and shoot, but that'd be a disservice to it.

After all, there's no special mechanics to make the game any more complex. It's just your aim tracking and movement, a skill that remains relevant in most games you play today. It's usually hitscan or low-recoil so people can't even blame the guns for their skill issue. 

Our Directorate has had an RCL Division since 2022, when the Rapid Response Force was first founded. They lived up to their name and served us well before going dark in 2023 from inactivity.

Ofcourse, it's not like they went down quietly. After RRU was formally disbanded, the remnants of the fallen division quickly coalesced around Vibe as he founded their replacement– the Legionary Task Force: MOSS (LTF6). 

Like MOSS, they were resilient and kept their legacy alive with their wins. they may be a bit niche, but they're the boogeymen when it comes to playing with them. Like I said, their skills are universal to most gun systems. I can confirm this since we also have some RRU veterans in Ehrenwache and fighting them is like a bespoken hell.  

It was April 11th when OMOCEPTION was appointed as Chief of LTF6. This was also the same time where the Task Force began to hold funerals as a retirement ceremony, courtesy of Supiamco. The first one was for OMO's predecessor, Frostiest_Soup. 

Once of the first acts that OMO undertook during his tenure was commissioning a new uniform for the Division, a legacy that stands in his place. Along with that, he opened developer applications and started to collect feedback to improve the division. 

With all of this being done within a month, his stay with us was cut short by a sudden retirement. As much as it was disappointing, Supiamco quickly took charge and roused the division– a testament to his commitment. 

Like I said before, we are of legions and divisions. When one falters, another takes a step forward and assumes their mantle. LTF6 was founded as an act of defiance against the corpse known as the Rapid Response Force, so I'd be damned to see it fall so soon. For a division to die, the sparks of every man within would have to fall first. 

When it comes to burning, moss burns quite well. As I write this story, I see their sparks burning bright. Sure, it's not like moss can burn forever. But, moss grows. In line with the Task Force's traditions, OMOCEPTION's funeral is on April 23rd. 

I'd give you guys an invite, but it's an intra-division activity. Still, the efforts of each of these men are to be recorded. What good is a legion that doesn't know its history? May they achieve what they couldn't here. 

People come and go. Here at the LNN, it becomes our mandate to write it once they've added a thread into our tapestry. That's all I have for my beloved readers, but read up on Supiamco's words. Game recognizes game and his message was of the best ones I've read here. 

As always, thanks for tuning in! This is the LNN, signing off!
