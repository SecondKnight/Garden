What stood out to me the most was the University's statement on Self Defense, particularly this line; "you may use only the amount of force necessary to extricate yourself from the altercation." 

In my eyes, this can be interpreted in two different ways. The first interpretation being that of a textualist, to use the bare minimum amount of force to **immediately** extricate yourself. An example of this would be knocking the aggressor over and making a sprint. The issue here is that this allows the aggressor to get back up and pursue the student, ultimately delaying the resolution of this situation.   
  
The second interpretation abides by the spirit of the statement, to use the minimum amount of force to **incapacitate** the aggressor. An example of this would forcibly restraining the aggressor or knocking him unconscious. It's more of a "stand your ground" than the first interpretation, so an issue with this is that the student may either injury themselves unnecessary or cause irreparable harm to the aggressor.
