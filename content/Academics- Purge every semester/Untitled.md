When it comes to advice, I usually treat them like gentle suggestions– nothing more. It's out of a sense of hubris, but rather the belief that I know myself best. Even if that perspective is a limited one. Even then, I still remember a few words I once heard years ago from a teacher– an understanding that completed my own. 

In a place that turned education into a ritual, to memorize, he told that the purpose of education to comprehend and learn from everything. To find a lesson is everything, be it big or small. From our human bonds to our gait, we always gain something from it– even if we don't see it. Oftentimes, we unconsciously store this information and then connect the dots– a process we call enlightenment. 

His words were a seed, one that has now germinated into tens of thousands of words– a garden that continues to grow.
