Two days ago, I read a man's obituary. A stranger to me, yet a friend to those I know. So when they mourned, I questioned myself– what right do I have to mourn his passing? After all, I don't know him nor am I shameless enough to pretend. And so, this question resolved itself in time. I may not mourn beside those people, but it is within my right to mourn. 

We all know that death hasn't slowed a single step, that people won't stop dying
